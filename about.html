About Me

A bit about who I am, how I play and where I want to go.

My Story

I have loved soccer for as long as I can remember. I have played for Camden Tigers since 2020, starting in NPL 2 Under 13s and working my way up to the Under 21 Colts, and I was part of the Under 18 Division 1 team that won the competition. My goal is to keep moving up through the levels and one day play the game professionally.

Outside of playing, I have coached young players for five months, which taught me how to explain skills simply and keep a group motivated. I have just about finished the first year of my diploma at Western Sydney University The College, where I have been learning to plan, design and build websites like this one.

More About Me

I mainly play on the wing, on either the left or the right. I can also play attacking or central midfield, and up front as a striker when the team needs it. I try to work hard for the team, communicate on the field and stay calm under pressure.

  • Teamwork and respect for teammates and coaches
  • Turning up on time and giving full effort
  • Listening to feedback and getting better

In the short term I want to earn a spot in a higher-level squad. Long term, I want to play professionally and keep coaching young players along the way.
